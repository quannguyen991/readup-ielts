# SAT Math: research, current coverage, and Band 9 plan

Last checked: 4 October 2026. This is an internal curriculum map, not course content for learners.

## Khan Academy course observed

The public SAT Math overview currently reports **13 units and 111 skills**. Unit 1 is an orientation and has no exercises. The remaining 12 units are the same four SAT Math domains at three difficulty levels: Foundations, Medium, and Advanced. The domain lesson counts are 8 Algebra, 10 Problem-Solving and Data Analysis, 13 Advanced Math, and 6 Geometry and Trigonometry at each level; `(8 + 10 + 13 + 6) × 3 = 111`. This matches the screenshots supplied with the task and the public course index; it is not a 14-unit course.

The skills progress from direct application to multistep reasoning within the same domain. Each lesson page links an article, a basic example, a harder example, and practice. Public Algebra lesson material emphasizes preserving equality by making the same change on both sides, solving one/no/infinite-solution cases, and reversing an inequality after multiplying or dividing by a negative. The practice asks four questions at a time; Khan’s public prep guidance recommends at least three correct before leveling up. Unit quizzes break longer units into groups, followed by a unit test. The course challenge draws 40 questions across the course. The screenshots also show progress states for Mastered, Proficient, Familiar, Attempted, and Not started.

### Advanced Algebra sample inspected

The public Unit 10 exercise for **systems of linear equations word problems: advanced** uses a numeric student-produced response. Its visible prompt describes adult and child ticket totals together with ticket-sales revenue, then asks for the number of adult tickets. Some mathematical values were stripped by the public page reader, so this note records only the question form and does not claim a numeric answer. The underlying skill is to define variables with clear units, turn the two totals into equations, solve the system, then verify the requested variable against both constraints. Those solution steps are a Band 9 teaching recommendation inferred from the prompt structure, not copied Khan lesson text. Likely learner errors to guard against are mixing ticket counts with revenue, swapping which variable represents adults, and reporting the wrong variable. A separate public systems lesson explains substitution/elimination and how a system may have one, zero, or infinitely many solutions.

## Implemented in Band 9

- Added an additive 13-unit course layer on `/math`, with Unit 1 plus the four domains at Foundation, Medium, and Advanced levels. It is the default Path view; the original path and its set links remain available under “Bộ luyện hiện có”.
- Each skill row links to its existing Vietnamese theory lesson and a difficulty-matched bank round. The page shows submitted rounds, not inferred mastery.
- Added 30 four-question checkpoint quizzes (two for Algebra/Geometry and three for Data/Advanced Math at each level) plus 12 unit tests. Each unit test has at least three questions per existing Band 9 skill and rotates deterministically among that skill’s templates at the unit’s difficulty. Quiz IDs and other assessments are additive; old curriculum IDs and learner records remain intact.
- Enriched all 19 Math lessons with a prerequisite, a cue for recognizing the problem type, and a short summary. Each lesson now links directly to Easy, Medium, and Hard practice rounds.
- Unit test results below 75% stay marked for review and keep that unit as the suggested next step; reaching 75% or more marks the assessment step complete. This matches the public prep guidance’s 3-of-4 checkpoint threshold. Opening a lesson or unit never advances progress or implies mastery.
- No schema or Supabase migration was needed. No Khan Academy question, answer choice, solution, video, image, or lesson prose was copied into Band 9.

## Question-writing recommendation

The current pool has coverage at all three tiers, but 29 of the 57 skill-by-tier cells have only one template shape. For a useful first diversity pass, target **two independently structured templates per current Band 9 skill and tier**. That means 29 additional original templates; each template can produce many parameter variations, so this is not a count of 29 fixed questions.

No new question templates were added in this implementation. The 112 existing templates already have independent mathematical verifiers and seed-based checks; new templates should be written for the highest-need subskills, then verified independently before they enter the question pool. The new quizzes/tests reuse the already verified templates.
