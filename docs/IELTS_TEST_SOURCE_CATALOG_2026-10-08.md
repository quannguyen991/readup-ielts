# IELTS test source catalogue

Research snapshot: 8 October 2026

## Existing project inventory

The local-only practice bundle at `.local-private/ielts-ocr/source-tests.json` currently contains 108 records:

- 44 records with locally extracted content: Cambridge IELTS 8 (6), `VOL 10 KEY` (6; not verified as Cambridge), and Cambridge IELTS 17–20 (8 each).
- 64 records that link out without importing questions: Cambridge IELTS 10–16 and 21 (8 per volume: four Listening and four Reading).
- The Cambridge 17–20 material is marked local-only by the importer and is excluded from Git and deploy bundles.
- Duplicate content appears across many third-party banks; counts from different sites must not be added together.

## Import status and handling

- A source appearing online, being free to open, or being labelled “Cambridge” does not by itself grant permission to copy its full questions, passages, audio, or answer explanations into a public app.
- Current source links are suitable for an attribution and discovery catalogue. Full-question ingestion should be limited to material with an explicit reuse licence, written permission, or content authored for this project.
- Keep recent exam recalls labelled as reported/reconstructed practice; do not present them as official or use them as calibrated score forms.
- This catalogue records source locations and their self-described coverage. It does not claim that every page in every bank was individually verified, downloaded, or converted into the app.
