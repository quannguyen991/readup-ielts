NĂM HỌC 2020 – 2021 
 
 
Môn thi: Tiếng Anh (Đề thi Nói) 
Ngày thi: 18/01/2021 
Question 1. In some cities people can build and design homes and office buildings however they like. 
This is preferable to having strict controls over the design and construction of housing and office 
buildings. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this view? Use specific reasons and examples 
to support your answer. 
Question 2. Universities should accept equal numbers of male and female students in every subject. 
To what extent do you agree or disagree? Use specific reasons and examples to support your answer. 
Question 3. People should read only books that are about real events, real people, and established 
facts. Do you agree or disagree with this statement? Use specific reasons and examples to support 
your answer. 
Question 4. School teachers are more responsible for the social and intellectual development of 
students than parents. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Use specific reasons and examples 
to support your answer. 
Question 5. Some people believe that a college or university education should be available to all 
students. Others believe that higher education should be available only to good students. Discuss these 
views. Which view do you agree with? Explain why. 
Question 6. Society is based on rules and laws. It could not function if individuals were free to do 
whatever they wanted to do. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this view? Use specific 
reasons and examples to support your answer. 
Question 7. Some people believe that social networking sites are not doing enough to protect users’ 
privacy. Others believe that it is not the responsibility of the sites. Users should be more careful about 
what they make available online. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. Use specific 
reasons and examples to support your answer. 
Question 8. Some people think that schools should reward students who show the best academic 
results, while others believe that it is more important to reward students who show improvements. 
Discuss both views and give your own opinion. Use specific reasons and examples to support your 
answer. 
Question 9. Many people worry that innovative technology will supplant humans in all future 
workplaces, whether industrial, administrative or creative. Others, however, insist that while technology 
is an invaluable tool, it could never replace people. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. Use 
specific reasons and examples to support your answer. 
Question 10. In the past, shopping was a routine domestic task. Many people nowadays regard it as 
a hobby. Do you think this is a positive trend? Use specific reasons and examples to support your 
answer.

sentences and try to SPEAK as naturally as possible. 
Your talking time should not exceed 5 minutes. 
Good luck!

Question 1. Have you learnt a new skill recently? Can you compare the skills that are important 
nowadays with the skills that were important 20 or 30 years ago? Use specific reasons and examples 
to support your answer. 
Question 2. Do you often use products made in Vietnam? What are the advantages of manufacturing 
consumer goods in your own country? Use specific reasons and examples to support your answer. 
Question 3. Some people believe that individuals are born with leadership skills. Do you agree or 
disagree? Use specific reasons and examples to support your answer. 
Question 4. The number of children that read books for fun has dropped dramatically in recent years. 
What are the reasons for this? How can we encourage children to read more? Use specific reasons 
and examples to support your answer. 
Question 5. Children’s spending time online is actually a good preparation for them. Do you agree or 
disagree? Use specific reasons and examples to support your answer. 
Question 6. Cybercrime is in general on the rise. Is cybercrime an issue in your country? What can we 
do to prevent cybercrime? Use specific reasons and examples to support your answer. 
Question 7. Some people say that there should be limits to the kinds of photographs and videos that 
can be shared on social media. Do you agree or disagree? Use specific reasons and examples to 
support your answer. 
Question 8. Is it important for schools to teach children about their cultural background? Why or why 
not? Use specific reasons and examples to support your answer. 
Question 9. Some people say that children should learn to deal with winning and losing in competitions 
so that they can be better prepared for life. What is your opinion? Use specific reasons and examples 
to support your answer. 
Question 10. Social media is becoming increasingly popular amongst all age groups. However sharing 
personal information on social media websites does have risks. Do you agree or disagree? Use specific 
reasons and examples to support your answer.
