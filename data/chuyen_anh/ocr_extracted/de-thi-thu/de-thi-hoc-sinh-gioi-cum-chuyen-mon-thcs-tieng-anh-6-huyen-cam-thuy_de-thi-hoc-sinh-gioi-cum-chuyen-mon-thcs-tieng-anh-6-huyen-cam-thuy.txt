Write a paragraph (from 100 to 120 words) about your favorite sport. 
Marking scheme. The impression mark should be based on the following scheme: 
1. Format (2 pts) 
The paragraph must include a topic sentence, supporting ideas and a concluding sentence. 
2. Content (5 pts) 
Ideas are presented with coherence and cohesion, style and clarity. 
3. Language (3 pts) 
A provision of a variety of vocabulary, good use and control of grammatical structures, good punctuation and no 
spelling mistakes.
