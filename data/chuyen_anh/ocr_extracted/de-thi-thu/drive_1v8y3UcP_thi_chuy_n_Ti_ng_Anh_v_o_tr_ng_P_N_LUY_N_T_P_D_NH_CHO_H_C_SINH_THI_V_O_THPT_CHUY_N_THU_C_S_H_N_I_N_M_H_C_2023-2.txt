IV. WRITING 
 
Part 1 (Sentence Transformation) 
86. (minute) panic kicked in 
If someone started panicking, it means panic kicked in (started to take effect). 
87. the odds of getting 
The odds of getting a well-paid job are the chances of getting it. 
88. been a real help 
You’ve both been really helpful in the original sentence means the same as You’ve both been a real help in the 
other; be a real help is an expression in which the indefinite article a is used before an uncountable noun help 
as the reference is to a specific context strengthened by the use of an adjective (real). 
89. you top up your 
If you top up your pre-paid phone, yoy buy more credit which enables you to make more phone calls. 
90. stepping outside her comfort 
To step outside one’s comfort zone means to decide to experience a new situation that one is unfamiliar with 
and which could feel uncomfortable.
